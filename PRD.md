# PRD — Video Editing Portfolio Website

## 1. Overview

A **premium, modern, minimal, high-converting portfolio website** for a professional video editing brand. The site must feel like a **high-end creative agency** (Apple / Stripe / Linear / MotionVFX / Frame.io / Artlist level), not a freelancer page.

**Goal:** Within the first 5 seconds of landing, a visitor should feel they are hiring a professional editor capable of working with premium brands and high-value international clients.

## 2. Objectives

- Build trust instantly with international clients & agencies.
- Convert visitors into leads (Book a Call / Contact form).
- Showcase work across all editing niches with filterable portfolio.
- Fast loading, SEO optimized, fully responsive, dark & light mode.

## 3. Target Users

| Audience | What they want |
|---|---|
| YouTube Creators | High-retention long form + Shorts editing |
| Startups & SaaS | Product ads, commercials, brand videos |
| Agencies | A reliable white-label editing partner |
| E-commerce Brands | Product ads that convert (FB/IG/TikTok) |
| Real Estate | Property tours, drone & luxury home videos |
| Corporate | Company profiles, events, interviews, training |

## 4. Core Features (Sections)

1. **Hero** — cinematic background video (autoplay, muted, loop), headline *"Turning Raw Footage Into High-Converting Content"*, subheading, CTAs: *View Portfolio* + *Book a Call*.
2. **Trusted By** — client logos / placeholders (YouTube Creators, Startups, Agencies, E-commerce Brands).
3. **Services** — 6 premium hover-animated cards: Long Form, Short Form, Commercial, Real Estate, Corporate, Motion Graphics.
4. **Portfolio Categories** — filter buttons: All, Ads, Vlogs, YouTube Videos, Shorts, Podcasts, Real Estate, Corporate, Travel, Luxury, Gaming, Education, E-commerce. Items open in popup/modal with: video preview, description, client goal, before/after (optional), software used.
5. **Featured Projects** — large premium cards: thumbnail, duration, client industry, services provided, results.
6. **Editing Process** — animated 5-step timeline: Receive footage → Creative planning → Professional editing → Revisions → Final delivery.
7. **Why Work With Me** — Fast Delivery, Unlimited Creativity, High Retention Editing, Attention to Detail, Premium Color Grading, Sound Design, Motion Graphics, Unlimited Revisions.
8. **Software** — animated icons: Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop, Illustrator, Audition, DaVinci Resolve.
9. **Testimonials** — video testimonials preferred; fallback: premium testimonial cards.
10. **Statistics** — animated counters: 100+ Projects, 50+ Happy Clients, 5+ Countries, 3+ Years Experience.
11. **FAQ** — accordion: turnaround time, file formats, revisions, editing style, thumbnails.
12. **Contact** — premium form: Name, Email, Project Type, Budget, Deadline, Project Details. CTA: *"Let's Create Something Amazing"*.
13. **Footer** — quick links, social icons (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Behance, Email).

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

- Responsive: Desktop / Tablet / Mobile.
- Fast loading (lazy-loaded videos & images, optimized assets).
- SEO optimized (meta tags, OG tags, semantic HTML, sitemap).
- Dark & Light mode.
- Clean, maintainable code; editable portfolio data (CMS-ready data layer).
- Smooth scrolling, fade/reveal animations, hover effects, magnetic buttons, animated cursor, glassmorphism cards, micro-interactions, premium page transitions.

## 6. Success Metrics (Conversion Focus)

- Visitor → "Book a Call" click rate.
- Contact form submissions.
- Time on page / portfolio video plays.
- Bounce rate under 40% on hero.
